Frequently Asked Questions

How does NammaRoute calculate the best routes?

NammaRoute uses advanced algorithms that consider multiple factors including real-time traffic data, public transport schedules, fare information, and user preferences to calculate the most efficient routes. Our system continuously updates to provide you with the most accurate recommendations.

Is NammaRoute available in other cities?

Currently, NammaRoute is focused on Bangalore, but we have plans to expand to other major Indian cities in the near future. Stay tuned for updates on our expansion plans!

How accurate are the fare estimates?

Our fare estimates are based on current market rates and official pricing from transport providers. While we strive for accuracy, actual fares may vary slightly based on factors like traffic conditions, time of day, and specific service providers.

Can I use NammaRoute without an internet connection?

Currently, NammaRoute requires an internet connection to provide real-time updates and route calculations. However, we are working on offline functionality for future versions of the app.
