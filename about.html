About NammaRoute

Transforming urban mobility through intelligent multi-modal route planning

Our Mission

NammaRoute was born from a simple observation: urban transportation is fragmented and confusing. With multiple options available—buses, metro, auto-rickshaws, bike taxis, and more—commuters often struggle to find the optimal route that balances time, cost, and convenience.

Our mission is to simplify urban mobility by providing intelligent, multi-modal route planning that helps people navigate cities efficiently, economically, and sustainably.

We believe that better transportation leads to better cities—with less congestion, lower emissions, and more time for people to spend on what matters most.
